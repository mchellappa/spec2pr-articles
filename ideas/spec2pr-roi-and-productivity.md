# Spec2PR: Proving AI ROI to Leadership — The Numbers Behind 5.5 Hours Saved Per Sprint

Audience: Engineering leaders, CTOs, and engineering managers making the case for AI investment
Tone: Data-driven and pragmatic — evidence over enthusiasm

Claiming AI saves time is easy. Proving it to a CFO or VP of Engineering requires
numbers, methodology, and a tracking system they can trust. This article explains
how the Spec2PR productivity dashboard was built into the DevEx AI Assistant extension —
tracking time saved per project, ROI calculations, and adoption metrics — and how
we validated the 5.5-hour savings figure that justified continued investment and
wider rollout.

Key points:
- What metrics actually matter to leadership vs what engineers care about
- How the local telemetry system tracks time saved without sending data to the cloud
- The methodology behind the 5.5 hours saved per project calculation
- ROI math: from single engineer savings to team and organizational impact
- How to use the productivity dashboard to build an AI investment case for leadership
